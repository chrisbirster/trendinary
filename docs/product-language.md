# Trendinary Product Language

## Brand position

**Trendinary**  
**The live dictionary of the internet.**  
**What's happening — and why.**

Trendinary should feel like the internet explaining itself: fast, clever, useful, slightly irreverent, but trustworthy underneath.

## Signature vocabulary

### SCAN
What the system does. Trendinary scans public internet signals for unusual changes in attention.

### PEEP 👀
The emerging-signals surface.

> See it before it blows up.

### WTF?
**Why's This Trending?** The instant explanation button on every trend.

### VIBE
A qualitative summary of the shape of the conversation: excited, skeptical, confused, angry, amused, divided, etc. It should be presented as a summary rather than fake scientific precision.

### LORE
The persistent backstory: origin, prior events, context, key people, prior peaks, and why the subject matters beyond today's spike.

### FOMO
The catch-up surface.

> You logged off. We kept score.

### Trendinary Score
A recognizable 0–100 measure of trend strength driven conceptually by attention, velocity, breadth, and novelty.

## Lifecycle vocabulary

- **Emerging** — small but accelerating unusually fast.
- **Rising** — rapidly gaining meaningful attention.
- **Breaking** — broad, fast, cross-source attention.
- **Peaking** — near maximum current attention.
- **Cooling** — attention is declining.
- **Resurfacing** — an older subject has become relevant again.

## Voice examples

Good:

- “Something weird is happening around AT Protocol.”
- “Popularity is late. PEEP watches the slope.”
- “Three things mattered while you were gone.”
- “This is spreading from developer circles into mainstream tech coverage.”
- “Get the lore.”

Avoid:

- “AI-powered trend intelligence platform.”
- “Leverage actionable social-listening insights.”
- overly confident sentiment percentages without a strong statistical basis;
- pretending an AI-generated interpretation is a raw fact.
